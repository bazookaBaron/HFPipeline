# touch storyboard (9:16, colourful iso kit, "It works with a hot dog")

Look: the approved colourful iso kit (mg/isokit) on the deep-indigo field: mint islands, coral / saffron / sky / violet accents,
glow ONLY on emitters (charge on the crossings, charge dots flowing, tap ripples, bulbs, the lit screen). Ember = "doesn't work"
(no tap, blocked charge). Saffron = charge. Sky = rows + water. Violet = columns + wool. Peach (coral mixed with cream) = finger.

**One world for cards 1 and 3-9:** a phone lying flat, screen up, on a mint island (a couple of pines, a little tent) seen in iso;
the camera moves across it. Under the glass is the sensor grid: 6 columns (violet) x 10 rows (sky), 60 crossings (a schematic:
a real panel has a few hundred; the tag says so). Each crossing carries a small saffron charge puck (height = charge).
The finger is a peach cylinder coming down from above the card (no hand, no person), the glove is the same cylinder in violet
knit (stripes), the hot dog is a coral sausage on a saffron bun. Card 2 is its own small world (three test circuits).
Times are global seconds of the 1.3x track (mg.js looks words up at run time with W(line, word)).
Sound-off test: every card shows the mechanism moving. No label-only beats, no templates.js, no grey cubes.

Card layout (9:16 `float`): x 54, y 154, 972 x 560 (bottom y 714); heads clear (headcheck.js: head-top of both actors never above
card bottom + 40 px). Cards enter/leave on the nearest shot cut. Text: every tag/label >= 28 px at 1080 wide (hud size 34 x .85),
at most ~3 words per tag. Max ~8 s per card. Under a card only MS/CU rigs (episode.json avoidUnderCards).
