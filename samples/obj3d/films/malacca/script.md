Facts and sources (approx. = estimate or rounded; all checked 2026-11-13):
- Narrowest point ~2.8 km (1.5 nmi), Phillip Channel, Singapore Strait (approx.). Strait length ~800-900 km (approx.). Wikipedia "Strait of Malacca"; RSIS paper on the Straits of Malacca and Singapore.
- Ships: record ~102,500 transits/yr (vessels >300 gt, Malaysian Marine Dept via Nippon Maritime Center, reported by Seatrade) -> "over a hundred thousand", ~280/day = about one every five minutes (approx.).
- Share of world seaborne trade ~21% by value 2024 (CSIS "South China Sea trade chokepoints"); 22-25% (Clarksons) -> "a fifth to a quarter" (approx.).
- Oil flow 23.7 million b/d in 2023, largest oil chokepoint by volume (US EIA, World Oil Transit Chokepoints, 2024 update) (approx.).
- China crude imports ~11.0 million b/d in 2024 (China customs via Reuters). Share via Malacca: ~80% in common use (Foreign Policy 2026), ~69% (EIA-based, CEPA), ~60% (CAS study 2018) -> "two thirds to four fifths" (approx., contested).
- "Malacca Dilemma": attributed to Hu Jintao, late 2003, speech to party officials (reported, no published transcript) (Chen, Univ. of Malaya ICS paper).
- US Navy logistics command (COMLOG WESTPAC) in Singapore since 1992; Changi Naval Base deep-draft pier can berth a carrier (US Navy SRF; GlobalSecurity).
- India's Andaman and Nicobar Command at the western mouth of the strait (established 2001).
- Piracy: ReCAAP 2024 ~62 incidents in the Straits of Malacca and Singapore, mostly petty theft (ReCAAP / Baird Maritime / Lloyd's List) (approx.).
- Suez: Ever Given, 23-29 March 2021, six days; ~369 ships queued (Wikipedia); ~$9.6 bn of trade held up per day (approx., KAPSARC / Lloyd's List estimate).
- Lombok/Sunda detour ~3-5 extra days (approx., analyst estimates, Asia Times 2026); Sunda too shallow for the largest tankers.
- Singapore port 41.1 million TEU in 2024, second to Shanghai (MPA).
- Myanmar-China pipelines (Kyaukphyu -> Kunming): crude design capacity 22 Mt/yr (~440,000 b/d) (CNPC design; some sources say 12 Mt); gas 12 bcm/yr (ERIA); crude throughput 2019 ~10.8 Mt = about half of capacity (ERIA / BRI Monitor). Opening years gas 2013, oil 2017 (commonly cited). 440k / 11.0m b/d = ~4% (approx.).
- Myanmar military coup Feb 2021, civil war since.
- CPEC headline value ~$62 bn (approx.; $46 bn at launch in 2015); Gwadar -> Kashgar ~3,000 km (approx.); Gwadar traffic a tiny fraction of Singapore's (~8,300 TEU 2025, one source); no China pipeline built.
- Kra Canal ~100-120 km (approx.); saves ~1,000+ km (approx.; 1,200 km figure may be round trip); not built; Thailand pitching a ~$28 bn land bridge (Fulcrum 2025).
- Northern Sea Route 2024: record ~3 Mt of transit cargo, ~92-97 transits, ~95% Russia-China (Rosatom / CHNL via gCaptain); open ~4 months.
  Malacca oil 23.7 million b/d x ~0.136 t/bbl = ~3.2 Mt per day -> the Arctic's whole transit year ~ one day of Malacca oil (approx., own calculation).
- China stockpile ~1.2 bn bbl, ~100 days of net imports (approx., Columbia CGEP 2025).
- Portuguese took Malacca 1511, Dutch 1641, British (Singapore founded 1819).
- China: world's largest crude importer (EIA); largest navy by ship count (US DoD China Military Power Report).
- Malacca Strait Patrol (Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore) since 2004. Khunjerab Pass ~4,693 m.
- Northern Sea Route needs ice-class ships, Russian NSR permits, frequent icebreaker escort (Rosatom).
- Japan, South Korea and Taiwan import most of their crude from the Gulf via Malacca (EIA chokepoints brief).
