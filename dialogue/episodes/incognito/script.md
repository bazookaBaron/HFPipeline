# "Nobody can see me in Incognito" (campfire, Leo + Walter, ~50 s final, voice at 1.3x)

Premise: Leo is sure that Incognito mode makes him invisible online. Walter, his grandfather, explains in plain words who still sees him. Both stay seated by the fire the whole time. The phone, the router and the websites exist only in words and in the MG.
Payload: (1) Incognito only makes YOUR device forget (history, cookies, form entries are dropped when the window closes); (2) the website still sees the visit and your internet address (roughly your town); (3) the router and the provider carry every request, like a postman who reads the address on the envelope (the inside can be sealed, the address is not); (4) the website, the router and the provider each keep their own record, so wiping your own copy changes nothing there; (5) if you log in, the site knows who you are; (6) a school or employer network can log it; (7) it is good for hiding things from the next person who uses your device.
Human question: "Is anyone watching what I do?" Turn: Incognito was never about hiding from the world, only from your own device's memory. Landing: the shoe shop still knows, the sister doesn't.
Running line: "sees" (L2, L4), "forgets" (L8, L9).

LEO (playful) [CU]: Grandpa, I use Incognito. Nobody sees what I browse.
WALTER (amused) [MS]: Nobody on your phone. Everybody else sees it.
LEO (surprised) [OTS]: Everybody? Who?
WALTER (warm) [MS]: The website, first. It gets your internet address, like any visitor.
LEO (curious) [OTS]: And the router?
WALTER (matter-of-fact) [MS]: Every request goes through the router, then your provider. Think postman. He reads the address on every envelope.
LEO (confused) [OTS]: Then what does Incognito hide?
WALTER (warm) [2S]: Your history. Close the window, and your phone forgets the visit.
LEO (thinking) [OTS]: Forgets. That's all?
WALTER (amused) [MS]: Like wiping the table. The meal still happened. The waiter saw it.
LEO (shocked) [CU]: Wait. Even if I'm logged in?
WALTER (firm, light) [MS]: Then you've told them your name. They file it under you.
LEO (curious) [OTS]: And at school? At work?
WALTER (matter-of-fact) [MS]: Whoever runs that network can log it. Incognito can't touch them.
LEO (curious) [OTS]: So what's it good for?
WALTER (warm) [MS]: Hiding a birthday present from your sister.
LEO (grinning) [CU]: That's all?
WALTER (dry, flat) [CU]: The shoe shop still knows. Your sister doesn't.

#end

Delivery notes (voice): normal conversational pace (the track is sped up 1.3x after, pitch kept). Leo: playful and sure of himself at first, surprised, then worried at the turn (L11 "Wait."), a grin on L17. Walter: warm, smiling in the voice, falls to a low finish; only the last line is dry and flat, after a beat.
Seated: no actions at all. Nobody holds a phone; the phone, router, postman, school and shop are words + MG only.
