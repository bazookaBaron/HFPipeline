# Sample short: "The Guard" (campfire · dadu + rohan · about 60 s · 152 words)

**Premise card**
- **Human question:** "I keep getting rejected. Am I doing it wrong?"
- **Who wants what:** rohan wants sympathy, or permission to quit. dadu wants him to stop
  sulking and see how doors really open.
- **The payload:** jobs open through people who know you, not only through applying. This
  is a theme, not a statistic, so there's no number to source in this sample.
- **The turn:** dadu didn't get the clerk job. He became the guard.
- **The landing:** keep showing up, and learn the guard's name.

---

ROHAN (flat): Forty-two applications. Forty-two "we'll keep your CV on file."

DADU (amused): Only forty-two?

ROHAN (annoyed): Dadu.

DADU (warm, pause=0.6): Nineteen seventy-one. The jute mill by the river was hiring a clerk. Shirt. Tie. A chair with wheels.
[[mg: map place="Hooghly river, Kolkata" label="Jute mills · 1971"]]

ROHAN (curious): Did you get it?

DADU: I went every Monday.

ROHAN: For how long?

DADU (thinking): Fifty-one Mondays. The guard knew my name before the manager did.
[[mg: number value=51 unit="Mondays" context="one gate, one year"]]

ROHAN (surprised): Fifty-one?- And then?

DADU (tender, pause=0.8): Then the guard retired. He told them, take the boy who comes on Mondays.

ROHAN (laugh): You became the *guard*?

DADU (amused): Best job I ever had. I saw everyone who came on Mondays.

ROHAN (thinking, pause=1.0): ...So I just keep showing up.

DADU (warm): Keep showing up. And this time... learn the guard's name.

#end · card: "Who's your guard?" (white on black, then "Follow for Part 2")

---

**Why it's built this way**
- **Hook:** a specific number with no greeting.
- **Subtext:** rohan never says "I feel like a failure". The number says it.
- **The payload is never stated as a moral.** "Learn the guard's name" carries it.
- **Callback:** "Mondays" opens the story and closes it.
- **Turn lengths:** 1–20 words, with one story turn.
- **No AI-cadence patterns.**
