# Script review 2026-11-15 (China, Trump, Nvidia miniature films)

The user said: "we need to improve the script writing also ... need to divide the video into chapters and then make the video". This is the review's chapter plans and rules for the rewrite. Writing bars: longform-writer skill, dialogue/SCRIPT-VOICE.md, dialogue/samples/campfire_guard.md.

## Plain language (user, 2026-11-15)
The user said: "the script looks like AI made it. You name people, but viewers don't know who they are. Keep it as simple and in layman's terms as possible."
- **People:** refer to them by role. Name only people most viewers already know, and say who they are the first time. Never drop a bare name.
- **Companies, agencies and technical terms:** say what each one is, or leave it out.
- **Language:** everyday words and short sentences, as if you were talking to a friend.
- **AI tells:** cut them. That means "Same X. Same Y." stacks, rule-of-three lists, dramatic one-word lines, "Here's the thing", "not X, it's Y", and fake suspense.

## Problems in all three films
- Each script has 9 to 11 `#chN` markers, so the "chapters" are 35 to 95 s fragments, not acts. Rewrite to 5 to 7 chapters of 75 to 110 s.
- The cold opens run 35 to 42 s and end on two questions. Cap the cold open at 20 s and about 55 words, with exactly one question.
- No object or person is followed through the film. Pick one and return to it in the payoff.
- Chapters end on flat facts. Every chapter must end on a re-hook: a question, a reversal, a stakes jump or a callback. Never go more than 90 s without one.
- Don't run more than three number sentences in a row.
- No "not X, it's Y" lines.
- Every "remember..." or "hold that thought" must be paid off.
- End card: after the payoff, the narrator says one short, plain like + subscribe line. The user asked for this on 2026-11-15, overriding the earlier "no spoken CTA" rule. The end-card buttons press on those words.
- Give every line a picture: a room or street scene with posed people, or a data graphic. Cut paperwork lines that can't be pictured.
- Add a `{dry}` deadpan beat every 45 to 60 s, punching up.

## Format for script_v4.md (keep the existing header: tempo, gap, chgap, length, fact block)
- `#hook` ... `#/hook` around the cold open.
- `#chapter <n> | <Title, 4 words max> | set:<set idea>`. The title appears in-world (a shop sign, a dossier tab, a street plaque), never as a typographic card.
- `{loop:key}` / `{payoff:key}` tags. The hook carries `{loop:hook}` and the payoff line carries `{payoff:hook}`.
- `{dry}` on deadpan lines.
- `#part` on chapter-end cliffhangers, so each film can also be cut into Shorts.
- `#payoff` replaces `#end`: a two-beat ending plus one forward claim, with no CTA.
- Length: **about 1,450 to 1,650 spoken words for 8 to 9 min** (corrected 2026-11-15). The old "2,500-2,700 words" figure was wrong. Measured on the finished films, the effective rate including gaps and chapter pauses is 165-185 words per minute: China v4 had 1,577 words and ran 8:30, Trump v4 had 2,013 words and ran 12:05. Count the words before voicing (see MINIATURE-LESSONS item 22).
