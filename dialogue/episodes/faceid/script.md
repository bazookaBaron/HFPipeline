# "How does my phone know it's me and not my twin?" (campfire, Leo + Walter, ~50 s final, voice at 1.3x)

Premise: Leo wants to know how Face ID can tell him from a twin he does not have ("hypothetically"). Walter, his grandfather, walks him through it in plain words. Both stay seated by the fire the whole time. The phone and its dots exist only in words and in the MG.
Payload: the phone projects 30,000+ invisible infrared dots on the face; a camera reads how they bend over the nose, cheeks and chin; that makes a depth map (hills and valleys), not a photo; the phone compares it with the map it stored at setup; a flat photo has no depth; a mask is hard and the phone also checks that your eyes are open and looking at it; strangers get in about 1 time in a million; identical twins are the known weak spot.
Human question: "Is my phone really only mine?" Turn: it is not a picture of you, it is the shape of you. Landing: twins can fool it, so the answer to the question Leo asked is "not always".
Running line: "hypothetically" (L3, L17, L18).

LEO (curious) [CU]: Grandpa, how does my phone know it's me and not my twin?
WALTER (amused) [MS]: You have a twin?
LEO (playful) [OTS]: Hypothetically.
WALTER (warm) [MS]: It throws over thirty thousand invisible dots at your face.
LEO (surprised) [OTS]: Dots? Like a photo?
WALTER (matter-of-fact) [MS]: No. A camera watches how the dots bend over your nose, your cheeks, your chin.
LEO (thinking) [OTS]: So it draws a map of my face.
WALTER (firm, light) [MS]: A map of its depth. Hills and valleys. The phone stored yours once, and checks it every unlock.
LEO (curious) [OTS]: So a photo of me wouldn't work?
WALTER (amused) [MS]: A photo is flat. No hills, no map.
LEO (curious) [OTS]: What about a mask?
WALTER (matter-of-fact) [MS]: A good one is hard to make. And it wants your eyes open, looking at it.
LEO (curious) [OTS]: And my twin? Same face, same map.
WALTER (warm) [2S]: Strangers get in about one time in a million.
LEO (curious) [OTS]: And twins?
WALTER (matter-of-fact) [MS]: Identical ones can fool it. Apple says so itself.
LEO (grinning) [CU]: So hypothetically, my twin reads my messages?
WALTER (dry, flat) [CU]: Hypothetically, get a better passcode. And a better twin.

#end

Delivery notes (voice): normal conversational pace (the track is sped up 1.3x after, pitch kept). Leo: curious, a playful "Hypothetically.", surprised at "Like a photo?", a grin on the last question. Walter: warm, smiling in the voice, falls to a low finish; only the last line is dry and flat, after a beat.
Seated: no actions at all. Nobody holds a phone; the phone, dots, photo, mask and twin are words + MG only.
